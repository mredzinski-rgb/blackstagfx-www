About us

We support entrepreneurs who trade with the world

Black Stag FX grew out of practice: years of conversations with owners and finance directors of companies that export, import and settle in foreign currencies. The same questions kept coming back: who am I actually signing this contract with, is my bank charging me too much, and what is happening on the markets.

So we brought the answers together in one place. We help you quickly get oriented on a company and its finances, compare a forward rate with your bank's offer and follow the key economic news. The decisions are always yours.

We are starting in Poland. More markets are on the way.

Our principles
  • Facts, not promises: we show data and numbers, not verdicts.
  • Discretion: we never share who you are checking.
  • Simple: no jargon and no hidden fees.
